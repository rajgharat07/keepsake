title: Evening Walk to the Temple Gate
people: Dadi

After tea, Dadi walks to the temple gate and back. She counts one hundred and eight steps each way on her fingers. She will not skip the walk even in light rain — only lightning stops her. She likes to pause at the jasmine bush and tell whoever is listening what the sky smelled like the year Beta was born.
