title: The Neem Tree Promise
people: Dadi, Dadaji
tags: childhood, village, promise
---
When Dadi was fourteen, Dadaji carved their initials into the neem tree behind the old schoolhouse. He promised he would wait until she finished her sewing apprenticeship. She kept the scrap of green thread from that dress in a biscuit tin for sixty years. She told her grandchildren: "Love is not loud. Love waits under a tree."
