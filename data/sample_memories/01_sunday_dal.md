Every Sunday morning Dadi soaked yellow lentils before sunrise. She said the secret was not the spices — it was patience. One slow boil, a spoon of ghee at the end, and never rush the tadka. When Beta came home from university she always asked for this dal first. Dadi would smile and say, "Home has a smell. This is ours."
