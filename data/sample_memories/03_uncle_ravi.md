Uncle Ravi is Dadi's younger brother. He drives a blue scooter that rattles on every speed bump. He brings mangoes every June and refuses to sit until everyone has eaten. Dadi calls him "my storm" because he talks too fast and hugs too hard. If someone asks who always fixes the ceiling fan, it is Uncle Ravi.
