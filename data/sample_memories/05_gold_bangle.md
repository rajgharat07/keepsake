title: The Lost Gold Bangle
people: Dadi, Amma
tags: wedding, jewelry, family
---
On Amma's wedding morning a gold bangle slipped behind the steel trunk. The whole house searched. Dadi found it wrapped in an old newspaper under the sewing machine. She pressed it onto Amma's wrist and whispered, "What is meant for you finds you — even under dust." The family still laughs that every wedding begins with someone looking behind a trunk.
